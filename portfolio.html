03 — The Maker

About
Me

A
Filmmaker — Gothenburg, SE

I believe moving pictures can make you feel alive.

I'm a filmmaker from Gothenburg, and I created Keep Smiling Visuals with a simple belief: that the world is full of beauty worth capturing — in the small, quiet moments just as much as the grand ones. The smile between two people. The light through a window. The feeling of being completely present.

We're surrounded by content that tells us to buy, to want, to consume. I want to make something different. Films that don't just sell a product — they leave a mark. Ads that make you feel something real, that connect a brand to something deeper than a transaction.

Every project I take on is built around one question: will this still mean something after the screen goes dark? That's the standard I hold myself to, every single time.
